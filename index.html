Posted by the amber Team

  • 13 Nov: In #4 USA, the team warned everyone (@all) not to sign up for anything promoted in the group, after a member promoted a Zoom session the team said was promoting a business. The team added that amber helps students for free.
  • 14 Nov: Admins removed spam posts from all five of #3's groups.
  • 15 Nov: In #4, the team told members to ignore a "US visa lottery" post and not click its link.

Headline Takeaways

  • Country-group activity up 71%: 471 messages, compared with 275 last report. Almost all of it was in #4 (280) and #5 (159).
  • #4 UK was the most active group (131 messages): January 2027 intake deposits (e.g. Coventry, due 18 Dec), Edinburgh applications, and which Amber Scholar category to pick.
  • #4 USA had real guidance: a member listed universities with 100% need-based aid (Berea, Penn, Emory, Harvard) and explained Fulbright for Master's.
  • Community #5 keeps growing and talking: +142 members (now 504) and 159 messages. Its UK group discussed LLB entry marks, GKS and Erasmus Mundus.
  • Communities #1, #2 and #3 were nearly silent: 4, 9 and 19 messages.
  • /community-E (Community #5's link) has almost caught up with /community-D in Short.io: 838 vs 910 clicks.
Needs Attention
  • Unanswered questions: "Does amber offer free study consultation? I really need one" (#3 Australia, 15 Nov). In #5, members who already applied for Amber Scholar asked what happens next and when funds arrive; a student in #5 Canada has been asking for course and university help since 4 Nov.
  • Promotions and spam: a business-promoting Zoom session (#4 USA), a "US visa lottery" post (#4 Canada and Germany), a WhatsApp group invite link (#4 USA) and a scholarship-ads link (#4 Germany).
  • Traffic is falling: Short.io clicks −23% and GA4 new users −49% week on week.
